App submission requirements

Languages & tech

  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript. TypeScript is fine once compiled to JS.
  • WebAssembly from C, C++, Rust, Go, Zig or AssemblyScript (Emscripten, wasm-pack, etc.). SIMD and threads work.
  • Graphics: WebGL 2 or Canvas. WebGPU is not available.
  • Any framework or engine (React, Svelte, Phaser, PixiJS, three.js, Godot web export) if it stays within the size limit.
  • Static files only, with an index.html entry. No server code.
  • Not accepted: native binaries, Android/iOS apps, browser extensions.

Fits the car

  • Landscape layout that works at 773×601 CSS pixels with no sideways scrolling, and scales up for full screen.
  • Touch first: tap targets at least 44 px, no hover-only controls, no keyboard required. Gamepad support is a bonus.
  • Keep the top-left corner (about 110×60 px) clear. The hub's ‹ Apps button sits there.
  • Runs smoothly on the slowest cars (Intel Atom, 4 cores): aim for 30 fps or better.
  • Under 25 MB total; under 5 MB is recommended. Should load in about 10 seconds on LTE.
  • Sound starts only after the user taps.
  • Works offline after the first load (recommended).

How apps run

  • Apps open in a sandboxed frame. Pop-ups and leaving frunk.store are blocked.
  • localStorage and IndexedDB work. Prefix keys with your app id.
  • The page is cross-origin isolated: anything loaded from another domain must be HTTPS and send CORS or Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy headers. Bundling assets is easier.

Rules

  • Made for parked use. Nothing meant for use while driving and nothing that gets around the car's driving limits.
  • You own or have the rights to all code, art and audio. Open-source licenses are fine; include them. No copyrighted ROMs or pirated games.
  • No malware, crypto mining, hidden tracking, or collecting passwords.
  • Don't use Tesla's or anyone else's branding as your own.
  • No adult content.

What to send

  • App name, a one-line description, and a category (game, tool, media, other).
  • A link to the source repo, or a zip of the built files.
  • A square icon (SVG, or PNG at 512×512) and a screenshot at 773×601.
  • The license, and which controls it supports: touch, gamepad, keyboard.

Every app is tested in a real car before it's listed.
