SuperTux

Tux's jump-and-run adventure across Icy Island and the Rooted Forest, plus two bonus islands: about a hundred levels to run, slide and stomp through.

Game data

The game is about 300 MB. It downloads only what you play, a few MB per level, and keeps it on the car for next time. Download everything first if you'll be somewhere without a connection.

Controls on the screen: the pad at the bottom left moves Tux, the arrow at the bottom right jumps, the star next to it runs and fires (hold it to carry things), and the button at the top left pauses; tap the menus to pick. A controller works too: the D-pad or left stick moves, A jumps, X runs and fires, B goes back in menus and Start pauses. On a keyboard, the arrows move, Space jumps, Ctrl runs, Enter picks and Escape pauses. Options has a size setting for the controls on the screen. Your progress and options are saved on this car as you play.
