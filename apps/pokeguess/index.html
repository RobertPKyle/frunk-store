    How to play

    Guess the secret Pokémon. Type a name and tap it in the list. Each guess shows how close it was:

    • Gen, Height and Weight: right, or whether the secret one's is higher or lower.
    • Type 1 and Type 2: right, in the other slot, or wrong. A Pokémon with one type has none as Type 2.

    The Daily is the same Pokémon for everyone each day, with 8 guesses, and it's always a Pokémon's usual form (no Mega or regional forms). Win days in a row to build your streak and earn titles. A new one comes at midnight.

    Free play picks any Pokémon, forms too, from the generations you choose. Fewer generations, fewer guesses.

    Search tricks

    Type filters instead of a name to narrow the list. Put several in a row, separated by spaces:

    • gen:4 exactly, gen!4 anything but, gen>4 higher, gen<4 lower
    • Works with gen, type1, type2, type (either slot), height (m) and weight (kg)
    • type2:none lists Pokémon with one type
    • gen!4 type1:fire height<0.9 lists small Fire Pokémon outside Gen 4
