Craft 4K

Notch's Minecraft4k, the tiny Java game he wrote for a 4 KB contest in 2010, rebuilt in C++ by TheSunCat with the whole world ray-marched on the graphics chip: a 512 × 512 block world with trees, sun shadows and a day that lasts about a minute. Break blocks and build with seven kinds, mirrors included. The world is kept on this screen, edits and all, so it picks up where you left off.

On the screen: drag anywhere to look around, the circle at the bottom left walks, Jump jumps, and Break and Place work on the block in the middle of the screen (outlined in white); hold them to keep going. Pick a block in the bar at the bottom. Detail trades sharpness for speed. On a controller: left stick walks, right stick looks, the bottom face button jumps, the right trigger places and the left trigger breaks, the shoulder buttons change block. A keyboard and mouse work too: click the game, then WASD walks, Space jumps, the left mouse button breaks and the right one places, the wheel or 1 to 7 picks a block.

Game: Minecraft4k-CPP by TheSunCat and contributors (GNU GPL 3), based on Minecraft4k-Reversed and Notch's Minecraft4k, compiled to WebAssembly with frunk.store's changes for the browser: the compute shader redone as a WebGL 2 fragment shader, plus breaking and placing blocks, touch and controller input and saving. Source: /craft4k/engine/craft4k-web-src.tar.gz, built by tools/build-craft4k.mjs in github.com/RobertPKyle/frunk-store; see /craft4k/NOTICE. It includes GLM (MIT).
