Wolfenstein 3D

Wolfenstein 3D, the 1992 shareware episode

id Software's first-person shooter that started the genre: escape Castle Wolfenstein, floor by floor, with a pistol, a machine gun and a chain gun against the guards, dogs and officers, to the boss on the tenth floor. The free episode, Escape from Wolfenstein, in full: ten floors with their secret rooms and treasure.

Keyboard brings up the car's keyboard for your name in the high scores and the game's other keys (Y to confirm, 1 to 4 for a weapon, F2 to save, F3 to load). A keyboard plugged into the car works too: arrows walk and turn, Ctrl fires, Space opens, Shift runs, Alt and the arrows strafe.
