OpenTTD

Free game

Controls: tap to click, and drag the map with one finger (or, while building, drag to lay track, roads and more). Drag with two fingers to move the map while a building tool is picked, or to scroll a list; pinch to zoom. Press and hold a button to see what it does. The Ctrl button in the bar holds Ctrl down for the next taps, as the game's Ctrl+click needs. Text boxes bring up the game's own keyboard. A mouse and keyboard work as on a computer. The game in progress is saved whenever you leave it, and Continue picks it up; the game also saves itself every 10 minutes, and its Load Game menu has every save. Saves and settings stay on this screen.

OpenTTD 15.3 by the OpenTTD team, an open-source remake of Transport Tycoon Deluxe (GNU GPL 2), compiled to WebAssembly with small changes for the browser. Source: /openttd/engine/openttd-15.3-web-src.tar.gz, with the changes in /openttd/engine/openttd-web.patch, built by tools/build-openttd.mjs in github.com/RobertPKyle/frunk-store; see /openttd/NOTICE. Graphics: OpenGFX 8.0 (GNU GPL 2). Sounds: OpenSFX 1.0.3 (CC BY-SA 3.0). Music: OpenMSX 0.4.2 (GNU GPL 2), played by this page's own simple synthesizer. Their source is in /openttd/data/.
