Prince of Persia

Jordan Mechner's 1989 original: sixty minutes to climb out of the dungeons, past guards, spikes and chompers, and stop the Vizier Jaffar. Twelve levels, running on SDLPoP, the open-source port of the DOS game.

Hold Grab while walking to take careful steps; press it at the edge of a fall to catch the ledge. A jump while running clears wider gaps. Quick save keeps your place on this car (the pause menu, or F6 and F9 on a keyboard); the game's own save (from level 3) is in the pause menu too.

Game: Prince of Persia (1989) by Jordan Mechner, published by Brøderbund; now owned by Ubisoft. Engine: SDLPoP 1.23 by Dávid Nagy, Falcury and contributors (GNU GPL version 3 or later), an open-source port of the DOS game, compiled to WebAssembly with small changes for the browser: /pop/engine/sdlpop-web.patch, built by tools/build-pop.mjs in github.com/RobertPKyle/frunk-store; the source is at /pop/engine/. The game's graphics, levels, sounds and music are the ones SDLPoP ships. See /pop/NOTICE.
