BattleShip

BattleShip is a fan-made PC port of Super Smash Bros. for the Nintendo 64, built from a decompilation of the game, so it runs as a native program here instead of in an emulator. It needs your own cartridge's ROM: the US version 1.0.

Only load a game you legally own, dumped from your own cartridge. frunk.store doesn't provide, sell or share commercial games, and never keeps yours: the game's files are made from your ROM on this screen and stay here. The ROM itself isn't kept. You're responsible for the files you load.

Plays with a USB or Bluetooth controller (or a keyboard). Your ROM is turned into the port's game files once, which takes about half a minute. Escape (or Menu) opens BattleShip's own menu with its settings. Testing: please report how it runs.

Port: BattleShip by JRickey and contributors (MIT license for the port's own code), built on the ssb-decomp-re decompilation by VetriTheRetri and contributors, with libultraship (MIT) and Torch (MIT), compiled to WebAssembly with frunk.store's changes for the browser (tools/build-battleship.mjs and tools/battleship/ in github.com/RobertPKyle/frunk-store). Menu fonts: Montserrat and Inconsolata (SIL Open Font License 1.1). See /battleship/engine/ for the licenses.
