Plays Heretic games: Blasphemer, a free dark-fantasy game made by its community for the Heretic engine, and IWAD files from Heretic, Heretic: Shadow of the Serpent Riders and the Heretic shareware version. Add-on levels (PWADs) aren't supported yet. Games pick up where you left off: the game is saved to the "AUTOSAVE" slot (last page of the load menu) whenever you leave it.

Controls: a USB or Bluetooth controller, the on-screen controls, or a keyboard. On the screen, the stick walks and turns, ‹ › change weapons, the arrows by Item pick from your inventory and Item uses what's picked, and in menus the stick moves, Fire picks and Use goes back. On a controller: D-pad or left stick to walk and turn (with a right stick, the left one steps sideways and the right one turns), L and R to step sideways, the right face button or right trigger to fire, the bottom face button to use, the other two face buttons to change weapons, the left trigger to use the picked item, Select for the map and Start for the menu. Hold Select and press L or R to pick an item, or the fire button to use it. With a controller, the on-screen controls hide to make the game bigger; touch the screen to bring them back. With the Wings of Wrath on, ▲ and ▼ buttons show on the screen to fly up and down; on a controller, hold Select and press up or down.

Engine: Crispy Heretic, part of Crispy Doom 7.2 (GNU GPL 2 or later), compiled to WebAssembly with small changes for the browser. Source: frunk-apps.vercel.app/heretic/engine/crispy-doom-7.2-web-src.tar.gz. Game: Blasphemer 0.1.8 by the Blasphemer project (BSD 3-Clause license; see /heretic/blasphemer/COPYING.md and CREDITS.md).
