The Elder Scrolls

Arena

The 1994 original: imprisoned by the Imperial Battlemage, you escape and roam all of Tamriel to piece together the Staff of Chaos. Dungeons, towns and quests across the whole continent.

Daggerfall

The 1996 sequel: the Emperor sends you to the Iliac Bay to lay a king's ghost to rest. A world the size of Great Britain, factions, guilds, and the patched 1.07.213 version.

Controls

Both games are played with the pointer, as they were with a mouse. Tap to click. Drag a finger to hold the button down while the pointer moves (in the 3D view, that walks toward the pointer). Hold a finger still for a moment for a right click; keep holding and drag to swing your weapon once it's drawn. Keyboard brings up the car's keyboard, for names, saved games and the games' keys. Menu is the Esc key (save, load and options). A mouse and keyboard work too.

Game data

Arena is about 10 MB to download and Daggerfall about 150 MB, mostly its world and cutscene videos. Each downloads what it needs as you play and keeps it on the car for next time. Download everything first if you'll be somewhere without a connection. Saved games stay on the car.

Arena asks the cost of a spell now and then, the copy protection of its day: Spell costs shows Bethesda's list of answers.
