fheroes2

Heroes of Might and Magic II on fheroes2, the open-source remake of its engine: build your towns, lead your heroes across the map and fight turn-based battles, against the computer. It plays your own copy of the game.

Your game files

From a copy you own (such as Heroes of Might and Magic 2: Gold from GOG), send HEROES2.AGG and HEROES2X.AGG from its DATA folder, the maps (.MP2 and .MX2) from its MAPS folder and, if you like, the music from its MUSIC folder. A .zip of the DATA and MAPS folders works too (in the Files app, select both, long-press and choose Compress); the whole game folder, with its videos, is too big for one code. HEROES2.AGG alone plays The Succession Wars; HEROES2X.AGG adds The Price of Loyalty. Each phone code takes up to 80 MB: send the music with a second code.

Only load games you legally own. frunk.store doesn't provide, sell or share commercial games, and never keeps yours: they're stored only on this screen. You're responsible for the files you load.

Controls: tap to click, drag to scroll the map. Touch with a second finger while the first is down for the right mouse button, which shows what something is (hold it). A mouse and keyboard work as on a computer. Save from the game's own menu; the game also saves itself every turn (AUTOSAVE in its list of saves). Saves and settings stay on this screen.

Engine: fheroes2 1.1.17 by the fheroes2 Resurrection team (GNU GPL 2 or later), compiled to WebAssembly unmodified with its own Emscripten build. Source: /fheroes2/engine/fheroes2-1.1.17-src.tar.gz, built by tools/build-fheroes2.mjs in github.com/RobertPKyle/frunk-store; see /fheroes2/NOTICE.

Get files from phone

  1. Scan this code with your phone's camera.
  2. On the page that opens, choose HEROES2.AGG, HEROES2X.AGG and the maps (or a .zip of the DATA and MAPS folders).
  3. They show up here in a few seconds. Up to 80 MB per code.
