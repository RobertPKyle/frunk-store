Your games

Send a game as one .zip of its folder (on an iPhone: Files, long-press the folder, Compress), or pick all of its files at once. Point-and-click games from LucasArts, Sierra, Revolution, Westwood, Coktel, Adventure Soft and more, including Humongous's children's games.

Only load games you legally own, such as the files from a copy you bought on disc or from GOG or Steam. frunk.store doesn't provide, sell or share commercial games, and never keeps yours: they're stored only on this screen. You're responsible for the files you load.

Tap to walk, look and use. Tap with a second finger while the first is down, or turn on Right-click first, for a right click. Skip skips a scene (Esc), Menu opens ScummVM's menu with Save, Load and Options, and Type brings up the keyboard for naming saves. With a controller: stick or D-pad moves the pointer, the right face button clicks, the bottom one right-clicks, the left one skips, the top one types a period (skips a line of speech in many games), Start opens the menu and Select the game's own menu (F5). Games pick up where you left off: an autosave is made every minute and when you leave with Games, and Continue loads it.

Engine: ScummVM 2026.4.0 (GNU GPL 3 or later), compiled to WebAssembly with small changes for this page. Get games from phone

  2. On the page that opens, choose a game's .zip, or all of its files.
  3. Tap Done here when they've all arrived. Up to 80 MB per code.
