How to play

You're the mayor of an empty piece of land. Zone it for homes (R), shops and offices (C) and factories (I), join the zones with roads, and bring in electricity from a power plant with power lines. People move in when the zones have power and a road, and the city grows from there. Taxes pay for the roads, police and fire departments.

Pick a tool. Tap the map to place a zone or building. Roads, rail, power lines, parks and the bulldozer are drawn with one finger. Look tells you about a spot. Drag to move around the map (with two fingers while a drawing tool is picked), and pinch or use + and − to zoom. Map shows the whole city with pollution, crime, land value and more.

The bars next to the money show what the city wants more of: residential, commercial or industrial. Watch the messages at the bottom: they say what your citizens need. Budget sets the tax rate and spending; Evaluation says what people think of you.

On a controller: the stick or D-pad moves the cursor, A builds (hold it to draw), B is Look, the shoulder buttons change tools, Y and X zoom, Start pauses.

Your cities are saved on this screen every game year and whenever you leave.
