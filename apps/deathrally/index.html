Death Rally

Death Rally, the 1996 shareware episode

Remedy's first game: a top-down death race with machine guns, mines and turbo. Win prize money, upgrade your car in the underground market and climb the ranks toward the Adversary. The free episode has six of the nineteen tracks and three of the six cars, with Duke Nukem among the drivers.

Keyboard brings up the car's keyboard for your name and the game's other keys. A keyboard plugged into the car works too: arrows steer, Up accelerates, Down brakes, Enter fires, Alt drops a mine, Shift is turbo, Space the horn (change them under Configure).
