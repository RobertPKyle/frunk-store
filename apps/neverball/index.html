Neverball

Tilt the floor and roll the ball: collect coins to open the goal before the clock runs out. Eight level sets, from a gentle Easy set to the fiendish Nevermania, with high scores and replays of your best runs.

Fast draws fewer pixels, for the smoothest game. Sharp uses every pixel of the screen.

Controls

Best with a controller: its stick tilts the floor more finely than a finger. Touch works too, at any time.
