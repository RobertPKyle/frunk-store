Duke Nukem 3D is for adults: it has strong violence, strippers and crude language. The game's own parental lock hides the nudity and some of the gore.

Controls: on the screen, the stick walks and steps sideways, a finger dragged anywhere else turns and looks, FIRE shoots (and turns too, if you drag it), OPEN opens doors and pushes switches, JUMP jumps (and swims up), DUCK ducks (and swims down), KICK kicks, ‹ › change weapons, Item › picks the next item (medkit, jetpack, steroids...), Use item uses it and Map shows the map; in menus, arrows move and OK picks. On a controller: left stick or D-pad to walk (with no right stick, the D-pad and stick turn left and right), right stick to turn and look, L and R to step sideways, the right face button or right trigger to fire, the bottom face button to jump, the left one to open, the top one to change weapons, the left trigger to duck, Start for the menu and Select for the map. Hold Select with another button for more: Select + bottom ducks, Select + right kicks, Select + left uses the item, Select + L or R picks an item, Select + top goes back a weapon. A keyboard and mouse work too (click the game to look with the mouse). Games pick up where you left off: the game is saved as "Continue" whenever you leave it, and Continue loads it. The game's own menu saves in its slots as well.
