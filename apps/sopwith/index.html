Sopwith

The 1984 biplane classic. Take off from your airfield, shoot down the enemy planes, bomb their buildings, oil tanks and tanks, and fly home before you run out of fuel and bombs. Watch out for the birds and the oxen.

New to it? Pick Single player, then Novice: unlimited bullets and bombs, and the plane can't stall. The high score table counts only games against the computer.

Controls

Touch and a controller work about as well. The Touch / Controller button on the game's bar swaps the buttons beside the picture for a controller and back.

Nose up and down turn the plane around its own wings, so upside down, Nose up points at the ground. To turn around, climb into a loop, then Flip at the top. Speed up on the runway to take off. Land on your own airfield (slow and level) to refill fuel, bullets and bombs.
