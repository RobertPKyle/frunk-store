Text Adventures

The Zork trilogy

How to play

The game tells you where you are and what you see; you say what to do, in a few plain words: north, open the mailbox, take lamp, put the coin in the slot, kill troll with sword.

No typing needed: tap a verb, then tap words in the story to add them (tap Take, then "lamp"), and Go. The compass walks you around. Undo takes back your last move, and the game is kept after every move, so you can stop any time.

Draw a map as you go, and look at everything: examine things, read what has writing. inventory shows what you carry.

Your games

Plays Z-machine story files (.z1 to .z5, .z7, .z8, or .zblorb), which is all of Infocom's text games but the few with pictures, and thousands of later ones made with Inform. Free games are at the Interactive Fiction Archive (ifarchive.org) and IFDB (ifdb.org). Glulx (.ulx, .gblorb) and other systems aren't supported. Only load games you may play: free ones, or copies you own. Your games are stored only on this screen.

Your own games

Only load games you may play: free ones, such as those at the Interactive Fiction Archive, or copies you own. Downloading or sharing games you don't own can break copyright law.

frunk.store doesn't provide, sell or share commercial games. Games you load stay on this screen; when you send them from your phone they're encrypted and deleted from our server as soon as the car picks them up.
