Your Quake

Send PAK0.PAK and PAK1.PAK from the id1 folder of a copy you own (in a Steam or GOG copy, the original id1 folder, not the "rerelease" one). PAK0.PAK alone is the shareware episode. The mission packs work too: PAK0.PAK from their hipnotic or rogue folder. Music files named track02.ogg to track11.ogg are played if you add them.

Only load games you legally own. frunk.store doesn't provide, sell or share commercial games, and never keeps yours: they're stored only on this screen. You're responsible for the files you load.

Settings

640×480 instead of 320×240: four times the pixels to draw, so it may run slower.

Controls: on the screen, the stick walks and steps sideways, a finger dragged anywhere else turns and looks, FIRE shoots (and turns too, if you drag it), JUMP jumps (and swims up), and ‹ › change weapons; in menus, arrows move and OK picks. On a controller: left stick or D-pad to walk (with no right stick, the D-pad and stick turn left and right), right stick to turn and look, L and R to step sideways, the right face button or right trigger to fire, the bottom face button or left trigger to jump, the other two face buttons to change weapons, Start for the menu and Select for the scores. A keyboard and mouse work too (click the game to look with the mouse). Games pick up where you left off: the game is saved as "auto" whenever you leave it, and Continue loads it. The game's own menu saves in its slots as well.

Engine: Chocolate Quake 2.1.0 by Henrique Barateli and others, based on id Software's Quake source (GNU GPL 3), compiled to WebAssembly with small changes for the browser. Get files from phone

  1. Scan this code with your phone's camera.
  2. On the page that opens, choose PAK0.PAK, PAK1.PAK (and any mission pack or music files).
  3. They show up here in a few seconds. Up to 80 MB per code.
