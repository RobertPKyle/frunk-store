Cosmo's Cosmic Adventure

Cosmo's Cosmic Adventure: Forbidden Planet

Apogee's 1992 platformer in bright EGA with AdLib music. A comet forces Cosmo's family down on a strange planet and his parents vanish: find them, clinging to walls with suction-cup hands, bouncing on enemies and dropping bombs. Shareware episode 1, in full.

Controls

Best with a controller. The on-screen buttons work too: the Touch / Controller button on the game's bar swaps between them.

The game saves where the level began. Keyboard brings up the car's keyboard for the game's other keys (the main menu's letters, a number for one of the nine save places, your name for the high scores, which Start skips). A keyboard plugged into the car works too: arrows move, Ctrl jumps, Alt drops a bomb, F1 is the help menu with saving and loading, Esc quits.
