Blake Stone

Blake Stone: Aliens of Gold, episode 1

JAM Productions' 1993 first-person shooter, published by Apogee, built on id Software's Wolfenstein 3D engine with textured floors and ceilings added. British agent Blake Stone takes on Dr. Goldfire and his mutant army in the Star Institute, floor by floor by elevator: friendly informants to talk to, food and coins to collect, access cards to find. Shareware episode 1, in full.

Controls

Best with a controller. The on-screen buttons work too: the Touch / Controller button on the game's bar swaps between them.

Keyboard brings up the car's keyboard for your name in the high scores and the game's other keys (1 to 5 for a weapon, F2 to save, F3 to load). A keyboard plugged into the car works too: arrows walk and turn, Ctrl fires, Space opens and uses, Shift runs, Alt and the arrows strafe.
