The Kroz series, by Scott Miller

How to play

You are the face ☻. Gems ♦ are your life: every creature that touches you costs gems, and you lose when they run out. Whip the creatures around you, crack walls and grab what you find (more gems, whips, teleport scrolls, keys for doors) and take the stairs down to the next level. Somewhere at the bottom is the treasure you came for.

Tap where you want to go and you take a step that way; hold to keep walking. Whip and Teleport are on the side, with Pause, Save and Restore (three save slots per game). When the game asks a question, its answers show up as buttons. Keys opens a keyboard for anything else.

On a keyboard: the arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down, or U I O J K N M , to walk; W whips, T teleports, P pauses, S saves, R restores, Q quits. On a controller: the D-pad walks (two directions at once go diagonally), A whips, B teleports, Start pauses.

Leaving a game ends it, so save first. Saves and high scores stay on this screen.
