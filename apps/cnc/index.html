Command & Conquer

Tiberian Dawn

The 1995 original: lead GDI or the Brotherhood of Nod in the war over Tiberium. Both campaigns, plus skirmish battles against the computer, with the music and EVA's voice.

Red Alert

The 1996 prequel: the Allies against the Soviet Union in an alternate Second World War. Both campaigns, plus skirmish battles against the computer, with Frank Klepacki's soundtrack.

Controls

Tap to select and give orders, as with a mouse click. Drag one finger to draw a box around units. Drag two fingers to move the map, or tap the radar. Hold a finger still for a moment for a right click (it deselects). The Deselect button does the same. Keyboard brings up the car's keyboard, for naming saved games and the game's keyboard shortcuts; a save left unnamed is named for the date and time. The sidebar on the right builds; tap the game's Options button at the top for saving, loading and settings. A mouse and keyboard work too.

Game data

Tiberian Dawn is about 60 MB and Red Alert about 110 MB, most of it music. Each downloads what it needs as you play and keeps it on the car for next time. Download everything first if you'll be somewhere without a connection. The cutscene videos aren't included.
