Logic Puzzles

Forty puzzles by Simon Tatham and friends, each with endless new boards in every size. Tap to play. Progress is kept on this screen.

Controls: tap the board for the puzzle's left click, press and hold for its right click (or turn on Right-click so every tap is one), and tap with two fingers for its middle click. Puzzles that need numbers or letters show them as keys at the side. On a controller, the D-pad moves the cursor, A and B are the two select buttons, X undoes and Y redoes. Help in each puzzle explains its rules and controls.

Simon Tatham's Portable Puzzle Collection, by Simon Tatham with Richard Boulton, James Harvey, Mike Pinna, Jonas Kölker, Dariusz Olszewski, Michael Schierl, Lambros Lambrou, Bernd Schmidt, Steffen Bauer, Lennard Sprong, Rogier Goossens, Michael Quevillon, Asher Gordon, Didi Kohen, Ben Harris and Anders Höglund (MIT License: /puzzles/engine/LICENCE.txt). Compiled to WebAssembly with small changes for this screen: /puzzles/engine/puzzles-web.patch, built by tools/build-puzzles.mjs in github.com/RobertPKyle/frunk-store; see /puzzles/NOTICE. The help pages are the collection's own manual.
