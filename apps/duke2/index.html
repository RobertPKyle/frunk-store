Duke Nukem II

Apogee's 1993 sequel in full VGA, with AdLib music and Sound Blaster effects. The Rigelatins have kidnapped Duke to pick his brain for their attack on Earth: break out of their prison and fight back. Shareware episode 1, in full.

Save 1 and Load 1 work while you play; a loaded game starts again from the beginning of its level, as in the original. Keyboard brings up the car's keyboard for save names and the game's other keys. A keyboard plugged into the car works too: arrows walk, Ctrl jumps, Alt fires, F2 saves in any of the eight places, F3 loads, F1 is the options, H is help, P pauses.
