Send games to your car

Pick game files on this phone: NES (.nes), Game Boy (.gb, .gbc), Game Boy Advance (.gba), Master System (.sms), Game Gear (.gg), SG-1000 (.sg), Atari Lynx (.lnx, .lyx, .o), PC Engine / TurboGrafx-16 (.pce) or Mega Drive / Genesis (.md, .gen, .bin, .smd), zipped or not. They appear on the car screen in a few seconds.

    Files are encrypted on this phone before they're sent. frunk.store can't read them, and deletes each one as soon as your car picks it up, or after 15 minutes.

    Only send games you legally own, such as copies you made from your own cartridges. frunk.store doesn't provide, sell or share commercial games. You're responsible for the files you send.
